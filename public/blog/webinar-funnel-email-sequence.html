Funnels

The webinar funnel email sequence: registrations to show-ups to sales

Sofiat Adedeji Updated 8 Aug 20266 min read
Quick answer

A webinar sequence has three phases: confirmation and reminders before the event, a 'we're live' message at start time, and a replay plus offer sequence afterwards. Reminders boost attendance, and the replay sequence often converts as many buyers as the live session.

On this page

  1. Before the webinar
  2. After the webinar
  3. The funnel pages
  4. Segment by behaviour
  5. FAQ

Before the webinar

  • Instant confirmation with calendar links.
  • A value email a few days before: what they'll learn.
  • Reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before.
  • A 'we're live' email and SMS at start time.

After the webinar

  • Replay email to everyone, with a deadline.
  • Separate messages for attendees and no-shows.
  • Two to three emails handling objections and sharing proof.
  • A final reminder before the offer closes.

The funnel pages

Registration page, thank-you page, reminder sequence, replay page and order page. This Systeme.io build included a webinar registration funnel with reminders and a replay step.

Segment by behaviour

Tag people who attended, who watched the replay and who clicked the offer. Each group needs a different message, and tags make that easy.

FAQ

How many reminders should I send?

Two to three reminders before the event is typical: a day before, an hour before and when you go live.

Should I offer a replay?

Yes. Many registrants can't attend live, and a time-limited replay keeps urgency while serving them.
