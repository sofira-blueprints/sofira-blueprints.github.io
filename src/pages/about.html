My story

Why I started Sofira Blueprints

Most businesses already have the tools: an email platform, a CRM, a booking calendar. What they don’t have is a plan that connects them into one smooth customer journey.

When I started building email campaigns, I kept seeing the same pattern: flows that were half-built, campaigns sent without a plan, leads sitting in spreadsheets, and emails quietly landing in spam. The marketing was working. The follow-up wasn’t. Fixing that gap became my speciality.

Today I help e-commerce brands and service businesses set up email marketing and CRM automation that follows up with every lead and brings customers back, mainly in Klaviyo, HubSpot and GoHighLevel, with Mailchimp, MailerLite, ActiveCampaign, Keap, Kit, GetResponse, Zapier and Make in my toolkit.

I called the business Sofira Blueprints because that’s how I work: every system starts as a clear plan you can see and approve, and ends as a tested, documented build that you own completely.
