HubSpot vs GoHighLevel: which CRM fits a small B2B business or agency?

Sofiat Adedeji Updated 27 Oct 20268 min read
Quick answer

Choose GoHighLevel if you're an agency or service business that wants CRM, SMS, funnels, calendars, reviews and AI chat in one affordable, all-in-one platform. Choose HubSpot if you're a B2B company that needs mature sales pipelines, deep reporting, a huge integration marketplace and a CRM your sales team will grow into for years.

The short version

I build in both platforms every week, and I've migrated clients in both directions. The right choice depends less on features and more on how your business sells. HubSpot is built around a B2B sales and marketing team managing longer deal cycles. GoHighLevel is built around fast lead response for service businesses, and around agencies who resell the platform to their own clients.

When HubSpot is the better choice

  • You sell B2B with multi-step deals, several decision-makers and sales reps who need forecasting.
  • You need reliable attribution and reporting for leadership.
  • You rely on many other tools (ERP, billing, product analytics) that integrate natively.
  • You want lead scoring, lifecycle stages and sales handoff to be rigorous. See my HubSpot lead nurture case study.

When GoHighLevel is the better choice

  • You're a local or service business where speed-to-lead wins jobs: missed-call text-back, instant SMS and online booking.
  • You want reviews, funnels, calendars, SMS and email in one login, at one price.
  • You're an agency that wants to package automation for clients using snapshots and sub-accounts. See the review automation and snapshot QA case studies.

Can you use both?

Yes. Some clients keep HubSpot as the sales CRM and use GoHighLevel for SMS, booking and reviews, syncing contacts with Zapier or webhooks. It works, but two sources of truth need careful field mapping. If you're consolidating, follow a proper CRM migration checklist; my HubSpot to GoHighLevel migration moved 607 opportunities across seven rebuilt stages.

FAQ

Is GoHighLevel better than HubSpot?

Not universally. GoHighLevel is better value for agencies and service businesses wanting an all-in-one tool; HubSpot is stronger for B2B sales teams that need depth and scale.

Can I migrate from HubSpot to GoHighLevel?

Yes. Data can be mapped and migrated; workflows and templates are rebuilt; a reconciliation check confirms nothing was lost.
