Service FAQs

The practical details.

How much does email marketing automation cost?

It depends on the platform, the number of flows and whether copy and design are included. Every project is quoted after a free audit, so you only pay for what moves the needle. Options range from a single-flow Quick Fix to a monthly Growth Partner retainer.

Do you write the copy and design the emails?

Yes. Copy, design, segmentation and the technical build are all included unless you already have brand templates you want to use.

Can you fix automations someone else built?

Absolutely. Many projects start as audits of existing Klaviyo, HubSpot or GoHighLevel accounts. I repair, simplify or rebuild what isn’t working and document what I changed.

Do you need access to my accounts?

I’ll ask for user access (never your personal password) to the platforms in scope. Most tools let you invite me as a team member and remove access at the end of the project.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. The Growth Partner retainer covers monthly campaigns, optimisation, reporting and technical support across your email platform and CRM.
