The challenge

Ad platforms and the CRM reported different numbers, and browser privacy changes meant many conversions weren't tracked at all.

FIG. 02

The blueprint

I installed and verified the Meta, Google Ads and TikTok pixels, enabled server-side tracking on a custom domain, set the attribution model and a 30-day lookback window, and connected attribution to contact records so the CRM shows the source of every lead.

What was delivered

  • 3 ad pixels installed and verified
  • Server-side tracking configuration
  • Attribution model and lookback window
  • Attribution report
  1. TriggerVisitor clicks an ad
