A2P 10DLC registration in GoHighLevel: get approved first time

Sofiat Adedeji Updated 13 Oct 20267 min read
Quick answer

To text US numbers from GoHighLevel you must register your brand (your legal business) and a campaign (what you'll send and how people opt in). Approval depends on exact business details, a website with a privacy policy and SMS terms, a clear opt-in, realistic sample messages and opt-out instructions.

What A2P 10DLC is and why it matters

US mobile carriers require businesses sending text messages from regular 10-digit numbers to register. Unregistered traffic is heavily filtered or blocked, which means appointment reminders, missed-call text-backs and follow-ups silently fail. GoHighLevel handles the submission through its phone system, but the quality of what you submit decides whether you're approved.

Before you register: get these ready

  • Your exact legal business name, address and tax ID (EIN for US businesses), matching official records character for character.
  • A live website on your own domain with a privacy policy stating that mobile numbers aren't shared with third parties for marketing, and SMS terms.
  • An opt-in method people can see: a form with an unticked consent checkbox, keyword sign-up or a verbal script.
  • A business email on your domain rather than a free address, where possible.

Step 1: brand registration

In GoHighLevel's trust centre or A2P section, enter your business details exactly as registered. Mismatches between the legal name, tax ID and address are the single most common reason brands fail. Choose the correct business type and vertical.

Step 2: campaign registration

The campaign describes what you'll send. Pick the use case that matches reality (for example, customer care, account notifications or marketing) and write:

  • A clear description: who you message, why, and how often.
  • Opt-in flow: where and how people agree, with a link or screenshot description.
  • Two or more sample messages that include your business name and opt-out language, for example: "Hi Sam, it's Bright Smile Dental. Reminder: your cleaning is tomorrow at 10am. Reply C to confirm. Reply STOP to opt out."
  • Opt-out and help keywords: STOP and HELP responses.

The most common rejection reasons

  1. Business details don't exactly match the tax registration.
  2. Website missing, under construction or without a privacy policy.
  3. Opt-in not clearly described, or a pre-ticked consent box.
  4. Sample messages that don't match the chosen use case.
  5. No brand name or opt-out instructions in sample messages.
  6. Content in restricted categories without the right declarations.

In my A2P 10DLC case study, a client with a previous rejection was approved after the business details were aligned and the campaign and opt-in language rewritten.

FAQ

What is A2P 10DLC?

It's the US carrier registration system for businesses texting from standard 10-digit numbers. Registration makes your messages trusted so they're delivered.

Why was my campaign rejected?

Usually mismatched business details, a missing privacy policy or SMS terms, an unclear opt-in, or sample messages without your brand name and opt-out language.

How long does approval take?

Timelines vary with carrier review queues, from a few days to a few weeks. A clean, accurate first submission avoids resubmission delays.
