The 7-email lead nurture sequence for coaches and SaaS

Sofiat Adedeji Updated 6 Oct 20268 min read
Quick answer

A strong nurture sequence delivers value before it asks for anything: deliver the lead magnet, share a quick win, tell your story, show proof, handle the biggest objection, make a clear offer and finish with a last call. Add behaviour-based branches so engaged leads reach sales faster.

Three principles before you write a word

  • One idea per email. Short, focused emails get read; long ones get saved for later and never opened again.
  • Value first, offer second. Earn the right to pitch by solving a small problem first.
  • Let behaviour decide the pace. Someone who visits your pricing page twice doesn't need four more educational emails.

The 7-email blueprint

#WhenJob of the emailSubject line idea
1ImmediatelyDeliver the lead magnet and set expectations"Your guide is inside (+ what's next)"
2Day 2A quick win they can use today"Try this 10-minute fix first"
3Day 4Your story and point of view"Why I stopped doing it the hard way"
4Day 6Proof: a case study or testimonial"How [client] went from X to Y"
5Day 8Handle the biggest objection"'I don't have time for this' (read this)"
6Day 10The offer: call, trial or programme"Want help doing this for you?"
7Day 13Last call and a soft close"Should I close your file?"

For coaches, the offer is usually a discovery call or programme enrolment. For SaaS, swap the offer for "start your trial" or "book a demo", and add an onboarding sequence once they sign up.

Behaviour-based branching (where the magic happens)

A static sequence treats everyone the same. A blueprint adapts:

  • Clicked the offer or visited pricing? Exit the nurture, notify sales or send a booking link immediately.
  • Booked a call or started a trial? Exit and move to a pre-call or onboarding sequence.
  • No opens after email 4? Try a different angle or a plain-text email from you personally.

This is exactly how my HubSpot lead nurture funnel works: a two-day delay, a page-view check, and then either a sales task or a value-first email.

Which tools work best?

Coaches and creators often do well with Kit, MailerLite, ActiveCampaign, GoHighLevel or Systeme.io. SaaS and B2B teams usually prefer HubSpot or ActiveCampaign for the CRM integration and lead scoring. The sequence matters more than the platform, but the platform decides how easily you can branch on behaviour.

FAQ

How many emails should a nurture sequence have?

Five to eight over two to three weeks works well for most coaches and SaaS companies.

How often should nurture emails go out?

The first immediately, then every two to three days. Move high-intent leads to a faster, sales-led path.
